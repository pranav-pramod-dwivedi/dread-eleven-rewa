Dread Eleven Convenes High-Velocity Tactical Camp at Martand Ground

By DE Tactical Cell (Chief Analyst) • Published on 10 Nov 2024

Rewa — Dread Eleven (DE) have commenced their intensive tactical drills at Martand Ground No. 3, confirming a 43-man roster spearheaded by veteran captain Akhil Mishra.
