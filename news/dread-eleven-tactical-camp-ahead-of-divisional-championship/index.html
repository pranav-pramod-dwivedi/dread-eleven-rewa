Dread Eleven Convenes High-Velocity Tactical Camp at Martand Ground

By DE Tactical Cell • Chief Analyst

Rewa — Dread Eleven (DE) have commenced their intensive tactical drills at Martand Ground No. 3, confirming a 43-man roster spearheaded by veteran captain Akhil Mishra.

Coming off 13 verified tournament victories across 24 fixtures, Dread Eleven are sharpening their death-overs execution and powerplay containment.

"Our track record in Rewa speaks for itself," Mishra noted. "With Kuldeep Sen operating at peak velocity, Kumar Kartikeya controlling the middle overs, and Ashwin Das executing at the death, Dread Eleven possess the most balanced bowling attack in the division."
