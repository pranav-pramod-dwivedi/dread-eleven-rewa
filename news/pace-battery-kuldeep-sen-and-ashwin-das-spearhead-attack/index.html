Pace Battery: Kuldeep Sen and Ashwin Das Set Tournament Velocity Benchmark

By DE Bowling Cell (Performance Analyst) • Published on 05 Oct 2024

With 28 wickets combined across tournament encounters, the tandem of Kuldeep Sen and Ashwin Das has established itself as the premier fast-bowling partnership in the Atal Bihari Vajpayee Tournament.

Sen’s ability to generate steep bounce from a hard length on the Martand Ground turf complements Das’s pinpoint yorkers and slower ball variations in the slog overs.
