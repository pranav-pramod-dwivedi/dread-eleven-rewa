Pace Battery: Kuldeep Sen and Ashwin Das Set Tournament Pace Benchmark

By DE Bowling Cell • Performance Analyst

With 28 wickets combined across derby encounters, the tandem of Kuldeep Sen and Ashwin Das has established itself as the premier fast-bowling partnership in the Atal Bihari Vajpayee Tournament.

Sen’s ability to generate steep bounce from a hard length on the APSU Stadium turf complements Das’s pinpoint yorkers and slower ball variations in the slog overs.
