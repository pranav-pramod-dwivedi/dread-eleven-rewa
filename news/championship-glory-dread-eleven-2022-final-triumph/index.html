Championship Glory: Inside Dread Eleven’s 2022 Title Defense at APSU Stadium

By Vindhya Sports Journal • Cricket Historian

The 2022 Atal Bihari Vajpayee Memorial Tournament Final witnessed one of the most intense battles in Rewa cricket history, as Dread Eleven held off Destroyers in front of a capacity crowd at APSU Stadium.

Defending 183, Dread Eleven’s pace trio broke the opening partnership early. Despite resistance from Destroyers, Kuldeep Sen’s ferocious spell and Kumar Kartikeya’s economical four-over stranglehold clamped down the scoring, restricting Destroyers to 162/6 and sealing championship silverware for Dread Eleven.
