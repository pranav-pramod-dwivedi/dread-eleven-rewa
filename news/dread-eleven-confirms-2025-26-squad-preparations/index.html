Dread Eleven Confirms 2025–26 Pre-Season Squad Led by Captain Akhil Mishra

By DE Tactical Desk • Chief Analyst

Rewa — Dread Eleven (DE) have commenced their intensive pre-season tactical training at Martand Ground No. 3, confirming a 43-man roster spearheaded by veteran captain Akhil Mishra.

Holding a commanding 13–11 all-time advantage over rivals Destroyers Cricket Club (DES), Dread Eleven are focusing heavily on bowling discipline and powerplay containment.

"Our track record in Rewa speaks for itself," Mishra noted. "With Kuldeep Sen operating at peak velocity, Kumar Kartikeya controlling the middle overs, and Ashwin Das executing at the death, Dread Eleven possess the most balanced bowling attack in the division."
