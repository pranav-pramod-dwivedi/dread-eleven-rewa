Captain’s Log: Akhil Mishra Crosses 900 Runs with 47.6 Average

By RDCA Sports Desk • Statistician

Dread Eleven captain Akhil Mishra has entered elite territory, amassing 905 runs across 19 matches against Destroyers Cricket Club with 9 half-centuries to his credit.

Averaging 47.6 runs per innings, Mishra’s reliability at number three has formed the bedrock of Dread Eleven’s 13 derby victories in Rewa.
