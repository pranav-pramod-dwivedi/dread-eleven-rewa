Dread Eleven Seal 50 Overs Derby Glory: Inside the Tactical Battle at Martand School Ground No. 3

Under the intense scrutiny of the Atal Bihari Vajpayee Memorial Tournament, Dread Eleven clinched a commanding result (Dread Eleven won by 2 wickets) on 04 Sep 2022 at Martand School Ground No. 3.

The 2022 clash at Martand School Ground No. 3 was defined early by the toss, with Dread Eleven electing to elected to bowl first on a tactical surface at Martand Ground No. 3 providing early moisture and seam movement. In the opening exchange, Destroyers Cricket Club posted 232/9 in 50 overs, anchored by Pranav Dwivedi's resolute 59 off 62 balls. However, Dread Eleven's bowling attack countered with relentless discipline, led by Mohd Arham Aquil who claimed 5/95 across 9 overs.

In reply, Dread Eleven answered the target with determination, scoring 234/8 in 50 overs. Akhil Mishra spearheaded the innings with 58 runs off 60 deliveries, while the fielding side tightened their grip through Pranav Dwivedi's crucial spell of 3/60. High-pressure middle overs created immense tension as every single and boundary was contested with derby ferocity.

Tactically, captain Akhil Mishra orchestrated field settings that suffocated scoring avenues through the cover arc and deep midwicket boundary. The bowling changes between overs 12 to 16 proved decisive, cutting off boundaries and forcing false strokes from Destroyers's middle order.

The match-winning honours went to Mohd Arham Aquil, awarded Player of the Match for 5/95 in 9 ov. This encounter not only shaped the 2022 standings of the Atal Bihari Vajpayee Memorial Tournament, but added another fiercely competitive chapter to Rewa's marquee sporting rivalry.
